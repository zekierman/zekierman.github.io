## Topic

Some apps feel good to use and some don't, and most of the time we can't say why.
My main idea: bad UI makes your brain ask questions, and good UI answers them before you ask.
Every question is a little bit of work, and less work for the brain feels good.

In the talk I compare real screens, two at a time: when will my order arrive, what is wrong
with my form, and what do these icons do.
At the end I look at AI-made web pages ("AI slop") the same way: they look familiar,
but they leave the questions open.
