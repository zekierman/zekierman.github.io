Some of these turned into products. Some of them only taught me something.

## Products

### Pandoo

*Mobile app · Konya · two of us on the software · [pandoo.novantesoft.com](https://pandoo.novantesoft.com)*

Cafes still run on paper stamp cards that get lost at the bottom of a wallet. Pandoo moves them to the phone: a QR code that rotates every 30 seconds, digital stamps, and a free coffee at the counter. Writing the app was the straightforward part; getting it through App Store and Google Play review was the real education. It has been live on both stores since October 2026.

### Novante Soft ERP

*B2B platform · two of us on the software · [novantesoft.com](https://novantesoft.com)*

Quotes, invoices, VAT, stock that decreases the moment an invoice is issued, and live exchange rates from the central bank. A multi-tenant platform that replaces scattered spreadsheets with one clean workflow.

### Erman Ofset

*Website · Konya · [ermanofset.com](https://ermanofset.com)*

My father's commercial printing house: Heidelberg and MAN Roland presses, around ten million printed sheets a year, and until recently no address on the internet. Before designing the site, I had to understand paper weights, the rhythm of the machines and what the trade actually is.

### Perdah *(in development)*

*Mobile app · React Native, Expo, Skia, Blender*

A journal for traditional wet shaving, built for enthusiasts and collectors. You log each shave, the razor, blade and soap you used, and how it went, region by region. A virtual soap in a nickel dish slowly wears down as you log, which turns a daily routine into something you can see.

## Tools for AI coding agents

### orkestra

*Claude Code skill · [GitHub](https://github.com/zekierman/orkestra)*

Coordinates several coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity) running side by side in terminal panes: one coordinator writes task briefs, collects the results through files and checks the claims before reporting.

### herdr-council

*Plugin · Go · [GitHub](https://github.com/zekierman/herdr-council)*

Asks every coding agent the same question, then lets a judge weigh the answers blind.

### kavra

*AI study skill · [GitHub](https://github.com/zekierman/kavra)*

A study companion for university courses that aims at two things at once: a top grade and real understanding. Works with Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity and Obsidian.

## Smaller things

### coffee-brew-tracker

*TypeScript · mobile · [GitHub](https://github.com/zekierman/coffee-brew-tracker)*

A tracker for brew ratios, grind sizes and pour history. I built it out of curiosity, months before Pandoo and long before I knew I would be writing software for cafes.

### akilli-galeri-sistemi

*JavaScript · zero dependencies · [GitHub](https://github.com/zekierman/akilli-galeri-sistemi)*

A management system for car dealerships, written without a single npm package. Partly because the problem did not need one, partly to see how far plain JavaScript could go.
