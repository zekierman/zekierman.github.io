I'm Zeki Erman, a third-year computer engineering student at Necmettin Erbakan University in Konya, Türkiye.

Most of what I know didn't start in a lecture hall. It started with a simple question: *"Can I build this?"* At first the answer was usually no. Then it became almost. Then it was something that only ran on my own machine, and eventually something people actually use on their phones. Most of what I can build today, I learned somewhere along that stretch.

## What I work on

- **Mobile apps.** I build with React Native and Expo. [Pandoo](/projects/#pandoo), a digital loyalty card for cafes, is live on the App Store and Google Play.
- **Software for real businesses.** A multi-tenant ERP platform and the website of my father's printing house taught me that understanding the trade comes before writing the code.
- **Tools for working with AI coding agents.** I spend a lot of time with Claude Code, Codex and Antigravity, and I write small tools that help them work together.

## What I'm doing now

- Building **Perdah**, a journal app for people who enjoy traditional wet shaving.
- Learning AI engineering from the ground up. Along the way I translated the README of *AI Engineering from Scratch* into Turkish.
- Writing this site as a project for my Professional English course. The [blog](/blog/) will grow from here.

## Tools I use

TypeScript, JavaScript, React Native, Expo, React, Astro, Supabase, Firebase, Python, Go, Skia and Blender.
